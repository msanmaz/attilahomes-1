About

Attila Utkucan

An independent realtor rooted in Istanbul with deep knowledge of the Bodrum and Istanbul markets. Attila has built his reputation on an instinct for potential — finding value where others see wear, and character where others see age. His clients trust him for honest guidance, local insight, and an eye that sees what a property can become.

Beyond brokerage, Attila is a hands-on developer specializing in the transformation of aging Istanbul apartments into modern living spaces. From sourcing neglected buildings in historic neighborhoods to overseeing every detail of renovation, he bridges the gap between vision and reality — delivering homes that honor their heritage while meeting contemporary standards.
